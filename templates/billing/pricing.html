No. Premium covers all supported exams and their available Mock and Practice sets.

What happens when Premium ends?

Your original Free exam and remaining Free allowance are restored. Your attempts, results, bookmarks and learning history stay saved.

Can I still use Previous-Year Papers for free?

Yes. Published Previous-Year Papers remain free and unlimited.
